
# Pizza Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

In this project, I analyzed one year of pizza sales data to understand revenue performance, product demand, and ordering patterns.

The goal was to identify useful business insights that could help a restaurant improve product planning, staffing, and sales decisions.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Key Results

- Total Revenue: $817,860.05
- Total Pizzas Sold: 49,574
- Total Orders: 21,350
- Highest Revenue Month: July
- Highest Revenue Weekday: Friday
- Highest Revenue Category: Classic
- Highest Revenue Pizza Size: Large

## Key Insights

- July recorded the highest monthly revenue, while October recorded the lowest.
- Friday generated the highest revenue among the weekdays.
- Large pizzas contributed the most revenue among the available sizes.
- Sales were strongest around midday and early evening.
- Revenue and sales volume varied across pizza products and sizes.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor popular products to maintain sufficient ingredient availability.
- Review low-performing products to better understand their demand.
- Consider busy sales periods when planning staffing and preparation.
- Continue tracking sales data to identify changes in business performance.

## Limitations

The dataset does not include profit margins, customer information, marketing campaigns, or discounts. Therefore, this analysis focuses on sales performance rather than profitability.

## Conclusion

This project helped me develop practical skills in data cleaning, data merging, exploratory analysis, and business reporting using Python.

The findings provide a useful starting point for understanding sales performance and identifying areas for further investigation.
